Starting Your Engaged Learning Experience

Moving From Planning to Action

After your team gets ready for the project to begin it will become less structured. There will be many other people to communicate with now. Not only do you have to keep up with your team but you also have to meet with your outside organization. From this point on you will want to make sure that you meet with your team and outside organization often. Keep communication open and accept feedback that you may not agree with.

Key Practices for a Strong Start

Meet With Your Team Every Week

You should meet with your team at least once a week. Although this can seem tedious it allows you to hold each other accountable to the task you are given. It allows you to keep a schedule and know if your team is comfortable with how the project is going.

Meet With Your Partner

Meeting with your team is not the only thing you should be doing. You should meet with your outside organization once a week as well. You will receive helpful information and advice from them. Something that you and your team will not be able to learn as quickly.

Listen To Feedback

There will be times where you will receive feedback that you do not like. But you should try to look at the positive side of it. Listen to your partners input and allow yourself to take different paths that you may not think of.

A Few Things to Keep in Mind

  • Meet with your team and outside organization once a week.
  • Keep good communication with your team.
  • Ask questions and clarify when necessary.
  • Listen to others.
  • Trust your team members.
