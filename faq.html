Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions During Your Experience

Every engaged learning project runs into a few of the same bumps along the way. Here are answers to some questions students often have once their project is underway.

What if my team doesn't know how to do what is being asked of us?

Your team may not know everything you need for this project. This is your opportunity to learn something new, ask other members of your team for help, and contact your instructor or partner if needed.

What if my partner company isn't responding to me?

Reach out to them. Send them a nice check in and let them know that you would like to continue working with them. Sometimes all it takes is a simple email to get things going again.

What if the scope of the project seems too large?

You don't have to do it all at once. Work on small things at a time and speak with your partner if there are any issues you would like to address. Have an uncomfortable conversation with them and work out a solution.

What if my teammate is not doing their fair share?

Remind them to do their work and offer to help them out if you can. Speak with your instructor if necessary to resolve the situation.

What if I'm not sure what my partner wants from us?

Don't assume what they want. Ask for examples or more information if you are unsure of what is expected from your team.

What if we are falling behind?

Re-evaluate your plan. Make sure to communicate with your team and partner. If you need to change things around, do so now.
